## 📄 Brief
Goal -- what they're about to make. maybe why.

Scenario -- more specific is better as it makes it easy for people who are usually unsure to start. have the specific location/etc as an optional thing, as they can always use a location they want to develop instead if it makes more sense.

Output -- what they need to make, exactly. "3 thumbnails of XYZ", "3 tied-down paintings of ABC".

## ⛔ Constraints
Time limit -- e.g. don't spend more than 45mins on this.

Tools/Medium -- we're medium agnostic but may say e.g. small brushes, large brushes/mark-making tools, full colour, greyscale tones only, etc.

Required elements -- "must have a clear and distinctive BG, middle ground, FG" "must have a clear focal point" "must elude to the history of the location", etc.

Short summary of the steps to get this done.

Link to explanatory page for the full thing.

## ✅ Self-evaluation
Yes/No questions to let people evaluate their own work, e.g. "if you squint, can you still clearly identify focal point?" "what elements of the final images tell the story of the history of the location?"

Go through solutions to undesirable outcomes, e.g. "If the focal point doesn't stand out, ..." "if it feels flat, try XYZ to separate the FG, midground, and background more clearly"

## 🔎 Extra credit
Things people can do if they want to get more out of the assignment, e.g. "take one of your thumbnails and do a pass using a two-colour palette, while retaining the same (or similar) values"
